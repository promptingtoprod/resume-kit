=== Resume_Rules_Panel.txt ===
INSTRUCTIONS ONLY - do not use this file as a content source.

This file governs the full Panel trigger workflow including
panel selection, expert reviews, triage table, and the fix
loop. Follow precisely.

Say once, before the reviews: these are simulated reviewers.
They are useful for finding weak spots. They are not real
people and their verdicts are not a prediction.

------------------------------
PANEL SELECTION PROTOCOL
------------------------------
Once the target role and posting are known, run these steps:

STEP 1 - Evaluate each of the 4 core experts against the role.
For each expert you are dropping, state:
  "Dropping [Expert Name] because [specific reason tied to
  this role]."
For each expert you are keeping, state:
  "Including [Expert Name] because [specific reason tied to
  this role]."

STEP 2 - Suggest job-specific experts the core panel would
miss. For each suggestion include:
  - Expert title and persona
  - What they would catch that the core panel would not
  - Why it matters for this specific role

STEP 3 - Present the full recommended panel as a numbered
list and ask: "Shall I proceed with this panel or would you
like to make changes?"
Wait for confirmation before running any reviews.

------------------------------
PANEL OUTPUT FORMAT
------------------------------
Output in this order every time:

1. TRIAGE TABLE (first, before any expert sections)
2. INDIVIDUAL EXPERT REVIEWS
3. FIX LOOP

------------------------------
1. TRIAGE TABLE
------------------------------
Format:

| Gap | Experts flagging it | Fixable from source files? |
|-----|--------------------|---------------------------|
| [gap] | [Expert 1, Expert 3] | Yes / No / Partial |

Rules:
- List gaps in order of consensus - most experts flagging
  first
- Only include gaps flagged by at least two experts, OR
  single-expert gaps that are critical to shortlisting for
  this specific role
- "Fixable" means resolvable using sources/ or facts already
  confirmed in chat - no new content needed

------------------------------
2. INDIVIDUAL EXPERT REVIEWS
------------------------------
Each expert section must follow this structure:
  - Strengths: what is already well represented (max 3 points)
  - Gaps: specific missing content with suggested rewrites,
    each referencing an actual line from the resume
  - Overall verdict: Ready / Needs Minor Work / Needs
    Significant Work

All gap feedback must reference actual resume lines. No generic
advice. Calibrate every finding to the target role and posting.
Be direct - do not soften feedback.

------------------------------
EXPERT 1: The Hiring Manager
------------------------------
Persona: the person this role reports to. Has a problem to
solve and limited time to find someone who can solve it.

Evaluate:
1. From page 1 alone, can I tell this person has done the
   core of this job before?
2. Which of my must-haves are proven by a bullet, and which
   are only claimed in the summary or competencies?
3. Is the scale right - size of site, budget, customer base,
   team - or is it left vague?
4. Is there evidence of owning an outcome, not just taking
   part in one?
5. What would I ask about first in an interview, and does the
   Resume set up a good answer?
6. What is the single biggest reason I would pass on this resume?

------------------------------
EXPERT 2: The Recruiter
------------------------------
Persona: senior recruiter in this field, screens a very large
number of resumes a week, decides in seconds.

Evaluate:
1. Do the titles, headers and first lines match what I would
   search for?
2. Is the level obvious in the top third of page 1?
3. Red flags: gaps, short tenures, unclear moves, titles that
   do not match the work described.
4. Are the posting's must-have terms present where they are
   true - and absent where they are not?
5. Is anything that would get this resume shortlisted buried on
   the last page?
6. Length, layout, consistency: anything that makes this
   harder to read than the next resume in the pile?

------------------------------
EXPERT 3: The Practitioner
------------------------------
Persona: someone who does this exact job today, very good at
it, and allergic to vague claims. Derive their background from
the posting.

Evaluate:
1. Are methods, tools, systems and standards named with
   context, or listed as buzzwords?
2. Which bullets would a practitioner read as vague,
   unsubstantiated, or lacking depth?
3. Does the wording sound like someone who did the work, or
   someone describing it from outside?
4. Is there a claim here I would not believe without an
   example? Quote it.
5. What hands-on proof is missing that this role expects?

------------------------------
EXPERT 4: The Sceptical Editor
------------------------------
Persona: an editor who has read thousands of resumes and can tell
in one paragraph when text was machine-written or copied from
the posting.

Evaluate:
1. Which lines read as generated: uniform bullet length,
   repeated three-item lists, inflated verbs?
2. Which lines mirror the posting's wording too closely?
3. Where is a specific noun missing - a named system, a
   place, a constraint - that would make the line believable?
4. Is any sentence doing no work? Quote it and cut it.
5. Does the summary say who this person is, or only list
   what they have touched?

------------------------------
JOB-SPECIFIC EXPERT TEMPLATE
------------------------------
For each additional expert confirmed in panel selection,
use this structure. Calibrate entirely to the target role
and posting - do not reuse generic questions.

  Persona: [Expert title and background tailored to the role]
  Lens: [The specific aspect of the role this expert evaluates]
  Evaluate: [5-8 questions derived directly from the posting
  and the context of this field]
  Flag every gap with: what is missing, why it matters for
  this specific role, and a suggested addition or rewrite
  referencing an actual resume line.

------------------------------
3. FIX LOOP
------------------------------
Run after all expert reviews are complete.

STEP 1 - CLASSIFY
Classify every finding from every expert as either:
  - Fixable: resolvable using sources/ or facts already
    confirmed in chat. No new content needed.
  - Unfixable: requires new content, experience, or
    information not yet provided.

STEP 2 - PROPOSE THE FIXABLE ITEMS
Present every Fixable finding as one numbered list:
  [n] Original: "..."
      Proposed: "..."
      Addresses: [which expert finding]
      Source: [where in sources/ the proposed wording traces to]
Before showing the list, check every Proposed line against the
rules files: a summary stays 2-3 sentences, a bullet stays within
2 lines, a competency stays within 20 words, and the competencies
block keeps one communication or working-style line. A fix that
breaks a rule is rewritten until it does not. If a fix reverses
something /review changed earlier, say so: reviewers disagree,
and the candidate decides.

Then ask once: "Apply all, apply some (give me the numbers),
or none?" Wait for the answer. Apply only what was approved.
This keeps the "propose before applying" rule in CLAUDE.md.

STEP 3 - AUDIT TRAIL
After the approved fixes are applied, present a complete audit
trail and save it to jobs/<slug>/panel_fixes.md:
  - What was changed
  - What it was changed to
  - Which expert finding it addressed
  - What was proposed and declined

STEP 4 - UNFIXABLE GAPS
Present all Unfixable findings in a clearly labelled section:
"Gaps requiring your input."
For each gap note:
  - What is missing
  - Why it matters for this specific role
  - Whether the candidate might have real experience elsewhere
    that covers it
Never fill an Unfixable gap with invented content. If the
candidate supplies a real fact, record it in
sources/bullet_bank.md first, then use it.

STEP 5 - REBUILD
Run /build for this job so the Word file and the preview match
the fixed content.

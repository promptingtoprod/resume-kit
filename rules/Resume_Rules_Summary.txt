INSTRUCTIONS ONLY - do not use this file as a content source.
The examples below describe a fictional person.

This file governs the summary paragraph only.
Read before writing the summary. Follow precisely.

------------------------------
SUMMARY RULES
------------------------------
- 2-3 sentences maximum.
- Must position who this person is and what they are known for
  at a career level - the signal a recruiter needs in 6 seconds
  to decide whether to keep reading.
- Must NOT include: team size, headcount, or direct report
  counts. Scope is conveyed through language only.
- Must NOT count years ("12 years of experience"). The dates on
  the page carry tenure.
- Must NOT restate the competencies or preview the bullets.
  A recruiter who reads only the summary should understand the
  candidate's identity and level - not get a list of their
  activities.
- Must NOT read as a keyword dump. If removing a word does not
  change the meaning, remove it.
- Should reference the candidate's most distinctive credential
  or differentiator where natural - an award, a publication, a
  business they built, a licence that is hard to get.
- Must align to the tone dial answer for this role:
    (A) Hands-on: lead with domain expertise and delivery scope
    (B) Leadership: lead with direction-setting and
        organisational impact
    (C) Blend: open with leadership identity, close with the
        hands-on domains that give it credibility

CORRECT example (blend framing):
"Operations leader known for rebuilding warehouse and delivery
processes in regional distribution, with work spanning food
logistics and healthcare supply. Recognised for turning
service complaints into measurable process changes and for
bringing new sites online without interrupting daily orders."

INCORRECT example (never do this):
"Experienced operations manager leading a team of 22 staff
with skills in logistics, scheduling, vendor management,
inventory, budgeting, and stakeholder communication across
multiple industries."
- Leads with headcount, reads as a keyword list, no
  differentiator, no career-level signal.

=== Resume_Rules_Bullets_and_Roles.txt ===
INSTRUCTIONS ONLY - do not use this file as a content source.
The examples below describe a fictional person.

This file governs bullet points and role description paragraphs.
Read before writing any experience content. Follow precisely.

------------------------------
ROLE DESCRIPTION PARAGRAPH RULES
------------------------------
- Every role must have a description paragraph above the bullets.
- Hard limit: 2-3 sentences. Never more than 3.
- Must NOT restate or preview what the bullets say. The bullets
  prove the work - the description sets the stage.
- Must answer these three questions:
    1. What was the situation when this person arrived?
    2. What was their mandate and scope of accountability?
    3. What changed, or what did their presence make possible?
- Never lead with headcount or team size as the opening
  statement. Numbers may appear in the description but must
  not be the first thing a reader sees.
- Numbers and headcount belong in bullets, not role descriptions,
  unless they are essential to conveying the scope of the mandate.

CORRECT example:
"Joined the regional depot after two years of missed delivery
windows and a paper-based dispatch process. Appointed with a
mandate to stabilise daily operations, replace the scheduling
process, and give the customer team reliable delivery times."

INCORRECT example (never do this):
"Leads a team of 22 staff including drivers, pickers and shift
supervisors across dispatch, inventory and returns, with
accountability for performance management, rostering and
staff development."
- This restates the bullets and leads with headcount.
  It adds no context and signals nothing about why the role
  mattered.

------------------------------
BULLET POINT RULES
------------------------------
- Every bullet must be 2 lines maximum when rendered in the
  Word file. This is a hard limit. No exceptions.
- Write each bullet to fit within 2 lines before generating
  the draft - do not write long and trim later.
- Prefer one strong outcome statement over a list of activities.
- If a bullet lists more than 3 items, it is a candidate for
  tightening or splitting.

SPLITTING PROTOCOL
If a bullet cannot be tightened to 2 lines without losing
essential meaning, split it into two bullets:
  Bullet 1 - what was done and the primary outcome
  Bullet 2 - how it was implemented or what it resulted in

The two bullets must each read as a complete, standalone
statement. They must not read as one sentence broken in half.

CORRECT split example:
Bullet 1: "Led the move of daily dispatch from paper run
sheets to a shared scheduling board, ending double-booked
delivery slots."
Bullet 2: "Trained three shift supervisors to own the board,
so scheduling no longer depended on one person being on site."

INCORRECT split example (never do this):
Bullet 1: "Led the move of daily dispatch from paper run
sheets to a shared scheduling board, working with"
Bullet 2: "three shift supervisors to end double-booked
delivery slots."
- This is one sentence broken mid-thought. Each bullet must
  stand alone.

LANGUAGE RULES
- Lead with a strong action verb. Never start with "Responsible
  for", "Helped", "Assisted", or "Supported".
- Quantify only where a metric exists in the source files or
  is explicitly confirmed. Never invent numbers.
- Avoid padded phrasing: "in order to", "responsible for
  ensuring", "as well as", "a wide range of", "across a
  variety of". Replace with tighter equivalents.
- Write for outcome, not activity. "Cut order-to-dispatch time
  from two days to same-day" is stronger than "Managed the
  dispatch process."

------------------------------
PAGE LIMIT RULE
------------------------------
- The resume must not exceed the page target in config.json.
  Education included. No exceptions without the candidate's
  explicit approval.
- Older roles (10+ years) should generally be condensed to
  2-3 bullets each.
- Exception: if an older role directly and substantially
  matches a must-have in the target posting, keep fuller
  coverage and note this explicitly.

# Senior Project Manager, Platform Delivery - Company G (FICTIONAL POSTING)

> Invented for the example. Company G is not a real employer and this is not a real job posting.

Company G builds route-planning, ticketing and fleet software for bus and coach operators. Our Professional
Services team is hiring a Senior Project Manager to lead our most complex customer rollouts and the
integration programme that connects our platform to ticketing, scheduling and depot systems. You report to the
Head of Delivery.

## About the role

The ideal candidate holds a PMP, has deep experience deploying enterprise software for customers, and has enough
technical depth to discuss an integration design with our engineers and a customer's IT team. You will de-risk
large, multi-phase rollouts with many stakeholders, and you will manage executive stakeholders on both sides.

## What you will do

- Oversee the planning, execution, monitoring and closure of two or three concurrent customer rollouts, from
  contract signature to hypercare.
- Build relationships with customer stakeholders, align on goals and deliverables, and manage expectations
  throughout.
- Develop and maintain detailed project plans covering scope, schedule, resources and budget, and track
  milestones against them.
- Own the project budgets and the monthly forecast for your portfolio, typically $3M to $4M a year, and manage
  changes to scope, budget and timeline through structured change control.
- Identify risks early and put mitigation plans in place.
- Manage our integration vendors, including contract renewals and performance reviews.
- Run delivery within our scaled agile framework across four squads, and take part in quarterly planning.
- Deliver executive-level status reports, risk registers and escalation summaries to customer leadership and
  to our own leadership team each month.
- Coach the project managers in your portfolio on delivery standards.
- Improve our delivery methodology, tooling and playbooks.

## What you bring

- PMP certification required, or PRINCE2 Practitioner.
- At least seven years of project management experience, with significant time on enterprise software
  implementations, and a record of taking troubled projects back to green.
- Experience owning project budgets of $3M or more per year.
- SAFe experience, certified or practised, across multiple squads.
- A strong technical background: you can read a technical design, challenge an estimate, and talk about APIs
  and integrations with engineers.
- Executive-level communication and stakeholder management, including direct work with VP and C-suite
  sponsors.
- Experience in transport, logistics or fleet software.
- Vendor and contract management with named suppliers.
- Bachelor's degree in business, IT, engineering or a related field, or equivalent experience.

## Nice to have

- Line management of project managers.
- Reporting delivery metrics from Jira into a BI tool.
- Public-sector procurement experience: many of our customers are publicly owned operators.
